These classic chocolate chip cookies are crispy on the edges and chewy in the center — a true crowd-pleaser.

## Ingredients
- 1 cup (2 sticks) unsalted butter, softened  
- 1 cup granulated sugar  
- 1 cup brown sugar, packed  
- 2 large eggs  
- 2 tsp vanilla extract  
- 3 cups all-purpose flour  
- 1 tsp baking soda  
- ½ tsp baking powder  
- 1 tsp salt  
- 2 cups semisweet chocolate chips  

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Line baking sheets with parchment paper.  
2. In a large bowl, cream together butter and sugars until light and fluffy.  
3. Beat in eggs one at a time, then stir in vanilla.  
4. In a separate bowl, whisk together flour, baking soda, baking powder, and salt.  
5. Gradually mix dry ingredients into the wet mixture.  
6. Fold in chocolate chips.  
7. Drop rounded spoonfuls onto prepared baking sheets.  
8. Bake 9–11 minutes, until edges are golden brown.  
9. Cool on wire racks and enjoy!
